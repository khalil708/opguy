Our Story

A World Tour
in One Café

As passionate coffee enthusiasts, we traveled the world discovering signature drinks that captured each region's culture and flavor. Those journeys inspired Let's Coffee — a cozy, travel-themed space where customers can enjoy authentic international flavors without leaving their city.

From our first store in Vinhomes Ocean Park, Hanoi, we're building a global brand that blends the warmth of a local café with the excitement of world travel.
